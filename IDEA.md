Canvas is a really confusing website and not all of the tasks or homework items that students need are in one place, sometimes they are on a mix of different sites. Students have so many different syllabi and so much information. We want to build a tool where users can put in their course syllabus and the app uses some string processing or some API type to scrape tasks and due dates and then export them directly to some sort of custom interactive dashboard calendar. We want to build this app that benefits students by keeping all of their homework, exams or due dates in one place with minimal manual intervention.
